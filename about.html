Why We Built This

Traditional file conversion tools often require you to upload your files to a server, wait for processing, and then download the result. This process has several problems:

  • Privacy Concerns: Your files pass through someone else's servers
  • Slow Processing: Upload and download times add significant delays
  • Cost Barriers: Many tools charge fees or require subscriptions
  • Accessibility: Offline use is impossible with server-based tools

We solved all these problems by building a completely browser-based conversion tool that processes everything locally on your device.
